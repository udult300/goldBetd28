FULL DESCRIPTION
Scaffale Lievito is a working shelf for breads and fermentation ideas: crust, crumb, preferments, and the habits that change them. Each entry stays practical enough to reread while dough rests.

Sort what you are studying, what already sits on your shelf, and what you want to revisit. It is a kitchen companion for curiosity, not a shopping list.
